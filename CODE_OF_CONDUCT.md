# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want participation in the Glyph project to be welcoming and respectful to everyone, regardless of experience, identity, background, or viewpoint.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and considerate.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Focus criticism on ideas, code, and behavior rather than people.
- Assume good intent while addressing the impact of harmful behavior.
- Respect differing experiences and perspectives.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, personal attacks, doxxing, sexualized attention without consent, and sustained disruption of project discussions are not acceptable.

## Enforcement and reporting

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards. To report abusive or harassing behavior occurring through GitHub, use [GitHub's report abuse and support channel](https://support.github.com/contact/report-abuse). Include links to the relevant issue, pull request, comment, or account and describe the behavior. For security vulnerabilities, follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) instead.

Maintainers will review reports, respect the privacy of people who report concerns, and take action they consider appropriate, including editing or removing contributions or restricting participation. This policy applies to project spaces and public interactions where someone represents the project.
